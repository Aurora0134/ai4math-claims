F3  truncated-subtraction counterfactual (Statement section)
======================================================================

The deleted sentence asserted that "truncated subtraction on N would make the defining
equations false as written". Tested directly: both recurrences iterated with monus
(a - b := max(a-b, 0)) in the two plausible association orders --
  order L: left fold, exactly as written term by term
  order G: positive terms summed first, then the negative block subtracted once --
against plain integer arithmetic on Z, over n <= 300:

  a3: Z vs monus-L diffs = 0 ; Z vs monus-G diffs = 0
  a4: Z vs monus-L diffs = 0 ; Z vs monus-G diffs = 0
  min value over the window: a3 = 2 , a4 = 3   (both sequences stay >= 0, so the
  subtraction never underflows and the two semantics coincide on this data)

The counterfactual is therefore false as an assertion and was removed. What replaces it
is the positive, checkable reason for the N -> Z codomain, in the same words the paper
lane adopted for its copy of the sentence (the sibling paper package's paper.tex:333-337, source repository, not shipped): the negative coefficients
let the equations be read as ordinary subtraction on Z, and the codomain makes the
Int.ModEq congruences and the Odd bridge usable without a coercion layer. Whether N
would have served is not a claim this note makes.
